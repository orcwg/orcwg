### Action Plan on cybersecurity and AI

Reference to the Action Plan → [Action Plan adda](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/eu-action-plan-cybersecurity-and-artificial-intelligence) 

* Published in July; divided into three pillars: making frontier AI safe/accessible, preparing the EU cyber ecosystem for the age of AI, and scaling European AI capabilities for cyber.   
* Key Action 6 focuses on a Critical Open Source Resilience Campaign, which is the primary area for open source community contribution.   
* ENISA is organising a hybrid workshop on vulnerability management in the age of AI in Brussels on November 25; registration is open until October 30\.  
* Registration available here → [Link](https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/CallForInterest_FOSSHybridWorkshop)

### ENISA Technical Advisory on SBOMs

* The first version was published last week; it is a high-level introductory text covering purpose, information types, formats, and technical choices.   
* The group views it as a starting point for those not yet using SBOMs, with opportunities to provide real-world integration examples and compliance scenarios.  
* Link to the [ENISA Advisory](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/enisa-technical-advisory-for-software-bill-of-materials)
