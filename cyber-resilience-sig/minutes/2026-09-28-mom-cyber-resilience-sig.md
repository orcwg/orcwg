### CRA Expert Group meeting and ongoing activities

- CRA Expert Group workstreams update \- the SBOM Reference Architecture is the one currently active. ENISA is leading this workstream; the community is actively contributing to non-public documents.  
- The next CRA Expert Group meeting is taking place on Oct 8th, and the agenda is available [here](https://ec.europa.eu/transparency/expert-groups-register/screen/meetings/consult?lang=en&meetingId=76324&fromExpertGroups=3967).  
- The key slots identified:  
  - **Manufacturer Tooling:** The European Commission will present projects funded via the European Cybersecurity Competence Center that developed tools for CRA compliance.  
  - **Single Reporting Platform:** An update on the first month of operations will be provided; the group seeks clarification on why open source was excluded from reporting.  
  - **Cybersecurity an** **AI Action Plan:** The Commission and ENISA will present an action plan on cybersecurity and AI \- previously commented in the working group [here](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/eu-action-plan-cybersecurity-and-artificial-intelligence).  
  - **Common Criteria and Conformity Assessment**: A key topic of discussion, but our participation in that area would be limited.

- Actions for all:
