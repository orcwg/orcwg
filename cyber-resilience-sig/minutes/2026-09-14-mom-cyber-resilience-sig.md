### Status of due diligence activities

- Check the [Github repo](http://www.github.com/orcwg/cra-due-diligence) → Multiple new issues have been raised; one or two PRs have been formally approved,  a milestone that had been a blocking point for some time.   
- Two work streams to be formally created:   
  - Integration of open source components for the first time  
  - Maintenance of open source components of CRA-regulated products when they are available on the market.  
- Scope of the work streams: One stream focuses on what due diligence means in the context of open source component management and the relationship with upstream projects; the other addresses legal obligations, including CRA compliance requirements for those placing products in the European market.   
- Planned releases/outputs: Beyond the full white paper, the group intends to publish smaller, more digestible documents that explain the approach being taken — these are seen as valuable standalone assets that are easier for external audiences to consume.   
- Ongoing call for contributors: More participation is actively welcomed

### Status of attestations, stewards and maintainers and FAQ

- Calls restarted: The stewards/attestation calls resumed last week (the Tuesday before this meeting) after a break, returning to the agenda that was in place previously.   
- Link to [due diligence](http://www.github.com/orcwg/cra-due-diligence): The relationship between attestations and due diligence is currently being discussed in both groups simultaneously; consolidating how attestations will be formally linked to due diligence is a priority for the next SIG call.   
- Attestations-Due diligence: Making sure all affected parties are aware of and not missing the priority-setting discussion is flagged as a specific agenda item for the next SIG call.   
- Single Reporting Platform (SRP), the stewards' position clarified:  
  - Stewards are not required to report exploited vulnerabilities through the SRP; if they attempt to do so, the report will be marked as invalid.   
  - The correct channel for stewards is through their national authority CSIRT, not the SRP directly.   
  - Stewards' formal reporting obligations begin next year, not September 11th, the deadline was not triggered as expected.   
- Feedback collection from SRP still relevant: Even though the deadline shifted, the working group still needs to determine how to receive information from manufacturers who are reporting through the SRP (e.g., about exploited vulnerabilities in open source components), and how to operationalise the links between the SRP and steward activities.   
- House of Stewards concept: A concept discussed before the summer break — consolidating all steward-relevant information, obligations, and tools into a single accessible resource. Mika drafted an update to the Steward white paper before the summer.   
- AI/CRA intersection FAQs nearly ready: A set of high-quality FAQs covering the intersection of AI regulation and the CRA have been contributed externally and are expected to be merged at the FAQ Task Force call the day after this meeting.   
- Official Commission FAQ updates merged: Following the September 14th non-event (stewards' obligations not triggering), the European Commission updated its official FAQ; those updates have already been reflected on the working group's FAQ website.   
- FAQ Task Force call scheduled: 15:30 CEST the day following this meeting; open to all interested participants.

### Code and Compliance Update

- Format: Full-day event, twice-yearly cadence going forward; one edition at FOSDEM (community-focused) and one standalone edition (externally focused).   
- Target audience shift: This October edition is explicitly designed to bring in external participants, particularly industrial companies and industry associations that use open source in their products but are not yet engaged with open source communities.   
- Strategic purpose: Use the event to bridge the gap between traditional industries and open source; demonstrate the working group's outputs; explain how to participate in working group discussions; and facilitate networking between open source practitioners and compliance-focused industries.   
- Agenda status: Almost finalised; expected to be published by end of next week. Confirmed content includes:  
- Registration incentive: A 50% discount code will be shared with working group community members. **ORCMEMBER50**  
- Sponsorship/partnership opportunities: Open for both manufacturers and not-for-profit associations, with different commitment structures depending on organisation type; promotional partnerships (no-cost, in exchange for community outreach) also available.   
- Feedback on event website: A participant noted the website is not yet clear enough for newcomers — the "who should attend" section and topic descriptions need to be more concrete and accessible for audiences unfamiliar with CRA/open source.
