### CRA Guidance final version 

- The European Commission's draft guidance on the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) provides critical clarity for the open source ecosystem regarding compliance boundaries, liability, and governance.  
- The guidance covers most of the points we raised in the process including the update on how to report vulnerabilities and contribute patches.  
- Manufacturers must upstream fixes, but a gap exists: no timeline mandated for voluntary upstreaming, raising concern that manufacturers may delay indefinitely until another party acts.  
- The communication and the documents can be found [here](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-new-guidance-support-timely-cyber-resilience-act-implementation).

### ENISA Updates Single Reporting Platform, Security by default and design playbook, vulnerability management

#### Single reporting platform

- ENISA Single Reporting Platform goes live September 11; obligations apply to both manufacturers and open source stewards. Key elements in the interaction with the platform  
  -  No API; all reporting must be done manually.   
  -  Only 2 accounts per organisation allowed; registration must be validated by the designated CSIRT.   
  -  No step-by-step user guide available; platform is heavy on clicks with no submission confirmation.  
- One aspect in which ORC wants to participate is in the collection of feedback from the open source community when interacting with the platform.
