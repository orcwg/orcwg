### Status of due diligence activities

- *Links for reference:*  
  - *Repository* \- [www.github.com/orcwg/cra-due-diligence](http://www.github.com/orcwg/cra-due-diligence)  
  - *Pull requests* \- [www.github.com/orcwg/cra-due-diligence/pulls](http://www.github.com/orcwg/cra-due-diligence/pulls)   
- Current Status: The group has moved from a "blank page" to active contributions in the repository, with several pull requests filed to define the due diligence method.   
- Core Objectives: The goal is to provide guidance for manufacturers on safely integrating open source components and demonstrating compliance with CRA requirements.  
- Key Debates:  
  - Scope: Discussion continues on whether the document should be a narrow white paper on Article 13.5 (integration) or a broader guide covering ongoing duties.  
  - Non-Technical Factors: There is a push to consider non-code aspects (e.g., supply chain security, vendor availability), though some argue these fall under NIS2 rather than the CRA.  
  - Contractual Due Diligence: Some members suggest using model contracts or attestations from stewards to satisfy due diligence, while others warn that the CRA may supersede such contractual clauses.  
- Next steps:  
  - Conversation will continue in the due diligence calls Thursdays  
  - Aspects related to open source stewards will be addressed in the Tuesdays calls.  
  - Things that overlaps, will go on Monday Cyber Resilience SIG calls.

### Code and Compliance Update

- Fall edition   
- FOSDEM edition of C\&C Thursday January 28, 2027  
- Fall Edition  
  - The fall Code and Compliance event agenda is taking shape with four thematic blocks:   
    - Institutional block; foundational/regulatory context
