### Updates on the FAQ issue backlog

* Apologies to those inundated with GitHub notifications.
* Tobie shared an update on the status of the backlog triaging. Most of it is done; the manufacturer FAQs are still remaining.
  * 78 [issues](https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/issues) were closed in the week before the call (notably 21 on stewards, 18 on manufacturers, and 16 on maintainers).
  * 57 FAQs were added, taking the total from 72 to 129, and 62 of the 72 existing FAQs were updated or moved.
  * 60 issues remain open, 33 of which concern manufacturers.
* Tobie also explained how he moved from a process with an involved AI pipeline earlier this year to relying essentially on a small skill that references the various READMEs ([CRA Hub](https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/blob/main/README.md), [FAQ contributing guidelines](https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/blob/main/faq/README.md)) and the [style guide](https://github.com/orcwg/cra-hub/blob/main/faq/STYLE.md), and leverages the FAQ and guidance [JSON API](https://cra.orcwg.org/api/v0/index.json) of the CRA website.
