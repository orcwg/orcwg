# ORC AI Policy & Compliance SIG Scope

# Purpose 

The ORC’s AI SIG supports open source stakeholders in their compliance work, primarily for AI regulations, but also for regulations that are not specific to AI but which apply to AI models and systems. While the initial focus is on the EU AI Act, the scope will expand to other global regulations based on SIG member interest.

The AI SIG supports its members in two primary ways:

1. Helping members understand and implement regulatory requirements.  
2. Interacting with public administration and agencies to shape regulatory instruments, ensuring they avoid unnecessary complexity, uncertainty, or burden, and that they remain compatible with the continued progress of open source software.

# Work on regulatory instruments 

Within the EU context, the ORC AI SIG will provide input to EU institutions during the drafting of regulatory instruments. This includes legislative acts (regulations and directives), their delegated and implementing acts, and the standardisation procedures and normative frameworks arising from them.

Outside the EU context, the SIG will adopt a similarly broad focus wherever there is sufficient interest from members.

The SIG will strive to maintain an active dialogue with relevant legislators and public administration bodies to effectively communicate the open source interests of the SIG members.

# Broader initiatives and context 

While much of the SIG’s work will be specific to individual regulations, it will also produce deliverables with a more general utility. Examples include:

* **Technical definitions:** Clarifying technical distinctions, such as when a product crosses the line from an AI model to an AI system.  
* **Position documentation:** Documenting the rationale behind past work (e.g., explaining why field-of-use restrictions in licenses can be problematic) to increase efficiency during recurring discussions.  
* **Unified compliance:** Collaborating with experts and other groups to examine overlaps between various legislative instruments and developing tools aimed at a unified approach to compliance.  
* Raising awareness among open source stakeholders regarding regulatory instruments of interest.

# Coordination with Eclipse Foundation groups 

Regulations may apply to AI without being exclusively specific to AI. If another Eclipse Foundation working group is already addressing such a topic, the ORC AI SIG will coordinate with them rather than duplicate efforts. For example:

* **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA):** ORC’s Cyber Resilience SIG leads CRA compliance. The AI SIG will leave general CRA topics to them, with one agreed-upon exception: the AI SIG will handle the specific question of how to apply CRA obligations to AI models.  
* **Sovereign AI Foundation:** The AI SIG will evaluate the Sovereign AI Foundation’s *PanEval* package as a tool for compliance work but the software development aspect of PanEval will remain with the Sovereign AI Foundation.
