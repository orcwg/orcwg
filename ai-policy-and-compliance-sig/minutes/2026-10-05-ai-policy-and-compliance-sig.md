## Notes

**Feedback to ENISA**

The group conducted a line-by-line review of the draft [SIG feedback for the ENISA consultation on secure-by-design AI-assisted software development](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PUViz9FUXTO3yJoqoxHASbqNqY7rnj0Rpt2aij0KB7Y/edit?tab=t.0) ahead of the October 15 submission deadline. Key points integrated into the document emphasize that the development environment forms part of the software supply chain, that controls must adapt to evolving agent autonomy, and that AI agents represent security-sensitive components. The discussion highlighted that mandatory human code review does not scale linearly with AI-generated output, requiring a clear distinction between internal proprietary workflows (where maintainers can enforce developer environment tooling) and open source models (where project oversight begins only at the pull request boundary). Following minor final edits to better reflect open source realities and skill usage, the revised text will be finalised and sent to the ORC Steering Committee for formal approval no later than October 7th EoB, so they can use the 5 business day window to analyse and approve it. The final approved text will be submitted to ENISA by October 15th.

**SIG landing page**

The Eclipse Foundation team presented a plan to build a dedicated AI SIG landing page for the ORC website to improve search engine optimization and serve as an accessible entry point for new community members. Similar to the existing Cyber Resilience SIG landing page, the content will break down high level overviews, key regulatory timelines, and the specific scope/impact of the AI policy and compliance on the open source ecosystem. A call for volunteers was issued to assist with content drafting throughout October, with web page implementation planned for November as a Q4 objective.

**Reminder: Code and Compliance. Potential requests from AI policy and compliance SIG**

Members were reminded of the upcoming [Code and Compliance](https://codeandcompliance.orcwg.org/) event taking place on October 27, 2026, in Brussels. The hybrid program is nearly finalized and will feature sessions exploring the intersection of the Cyber Resilience Act, the AI Act, and broader open source compliance challenges. All SIG members are encouraged to attend in person to foster face-to-face community alignment.

**Announcement: Eclipse Sovereign AI Foundation launched**

The group noted the recent official launch of the [Eclipse Sovereign AI Foundation](https://aieclipse.org/sovereign-ai-foundation/). While the SIG focuses on regulatory policy and compliance strategy, the Sovereign AI Foundation will concentrate primarily on software development coordination and aligning technical AI projects across different working groups. A representative from the Eclipse Foundation will be invited to present at a future SIG meeting.
