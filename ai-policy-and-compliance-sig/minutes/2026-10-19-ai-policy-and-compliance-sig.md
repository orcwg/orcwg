## Notes

**Preparing the AI policy and Compliance SIG Deliverable plan**

The group reviewed upcoming priorities and agreed to establish a formal deliverable tracking table on GitHub, mirroring the structure used by the Cyber Resilience SIG, to record document leads, status, and tentative deadlines. Key planned outputs include mapping CRA and AI Act model interactions into a structured matrix, outlining AI Act compliance timelines, contributing to the SIG landing page, and analyzing the European Commission’s Copyright Directive consultation ahead of its November 3 deadline.

**Status on SIG feedback for the ENISA consultation on secure-by-design AI-assisted software development**

An update was provided regarding the SIG’s submitted feedback for the ENISA consultation on secure-by-design software development. Due to an internal permissions issue that temporarily restricted Google Doc access during the ORC Steering Committee review period, the SIG requested and received a short extension from ENISA, with final submission scheduled for later in the week following standard ORC Steering Committee review.

**Contribution on the CRA and AI Act interactions table**

Postponed until next meeting as contributor was not present.

**Last Call: Code and Compliance**

A final administrative reminder was given for the upcoming Code and Compliance event taking place on October 27, 2026, in Brussels. Members were encouraged to register online and note that representatives from the European Commission’s AI Office and CRA team will be present, offering an opportunity to establish informal technical dialogue channels.

**AI Act standard published**

The SIG noted the official publication of the first harmonized EU AI Act standard in the Official Journal of the European Union. It was clarified that while publication in the Official Journal formally grants a legal presumption of conformity under EU law, the underlying technical text remains identical to the previously available draft standard.
