## Notes
Minutes of previous meeting approved

**Preparing the AI policy and Compliance SIG Deliverable plan**

Preparing the AI policy and Compliance SIG Deliverable plan (FAQ, Inventory, AI cat and CRA interplay white paper, etc.)    
The github pages for the deliverables plan were presented (([FAQ](https://github.com/orcwg/orcwg/issues/371), [Inventory](https://github.com/orcwg/orcwg/issues/372), [AI cat and CRA interplay white paper](https://github.com/orcwg/orcwg/issues/373))
There was lengthy discussion about the white paper's table mapping AI Act requirements to evidence. It was agreed that this is a good starting point for demonstrating how someone who is already working on CRA compliance can map that work to what is needed for AI Act compliance (to increase efficiency and spot gaps).

The feedback submitted to ENISA for their consultation on secure-by-design AI-assisted software development was submitted on behalf of the ORC WG on October 22nd. The final version submitted can be seen [here](https://github.com/orcwg/orcwg/blob/main/ai-policy-and-compliance-sig/coordination/enisa/Feedback%20to%20the%20ENISA%20technical%20Advisory%20on%20AI-assisted%20software%20development.pdf)

**Feedback: Code and Compliance**

The feedback received was positive on the presentations from Code and Compliance, which will be on the youtube channel soon.

**European Commission's Copyright Directive consultation**

Comments were requested on whether the Copyright Directive is relevant for the AI SIG.  Consensus was a quiet "no".

**AoB**

Note was made about the pushing back of AI Act deadlines and how companies have less urgency for AI Act compliance, but this also gives us time to prepare a framework.
Two CENELEC standards for the AI Act have been rejected internally in CENELEC (logging and risk)
Overview of the "Brussels effect" and how other countries are indeed adopting regulations that are similar or compatible with the AI Act, and how this is being done through dialogue during the drafting phase rather writing the legislation and then afterward using Mutual Recognition Treaties or other mechanisms.
