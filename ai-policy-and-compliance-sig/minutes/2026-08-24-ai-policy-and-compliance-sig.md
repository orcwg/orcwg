## Minutes

1. **Minutes approved**: for 10 Aug 2026

2. **Approval** of process to define scope: Discussion online until 21 September.  Will also be discussed in 7 September meeting, but for quite a few people this is a public holiday, so the main discussion will be online.  The scope can be changed later but it's useful for the next steps which are to define deliverables and expand membership.

3. The online discussion will be captured in a google doc: https://docs.google.com/document/d/17EjOCd4LjLunnDPB4MSDB51yfs53ceeECuqcm4g64cY/edit

4. There was discussion during the call for larger topics such as:
   - building a resource inventory
   - work on the question of how the CRA applies to software with an AI model.  This needs a systematic approach.  The EC is working on this but the outcome will be better if we help.
  
5. And smaller topics which could start as a FAQ entry and can grow if needed (see google doc, linked above)
   - There was a suggestion that we review the CRA FAQ to see which questions also apply to AI Act

7. There was discussion of the problems caused by licences which try to restrict use, even for a good cause.  This problem was in an early draft of the Code of Practice on transparency.  We managed to get it removed, but since this problem comes up again and again, we should consider documenting the topic so that we can easily contribute to future discussions.
